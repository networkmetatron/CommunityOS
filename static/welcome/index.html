Can't reach CommunityOS?

  1. Verify community.home.arpa resolves (try reconnecting to the network).
  2. Disconnect and reconnect Wi‑Fi, or unplug/replug Ethernet, or renew the DHCP lease.
  3. Install the CommunityOS certificate (button above).
  4. Refresh the browser (or open a private window).

After the server becomes your DNS, devices often keep the old DHCP lease until you reconnect.
